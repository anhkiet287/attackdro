How a research task moves through the lab

One task, top to bottom: from director intent, through Claude's plan and self-red-team, past the ChatGPT critic gate and the director's decision, into locked execution, audit, and back into the project state. Four roles, one per colored lane; striped nodes are the four hard gates; grey tags are folded-in steps, not separate agents.

02

The four critic gates

outside these · critic is silent

The critic step above fires only at these four moments — the high-cost, hard-to-reverse decisions. G4 is new in v2 because the planner now also authors the plan, making a direction change the point most likely to drift.

GateFires beforeWhy it existsRule
G1Locking a threshold / pre-registrationCommitment that cannot be walked back after seeing resultscritic Pass required
G2Launching >1 training run (or high-compute run)Spends real compute via the runnerdirector + Pass
G3Writing a final / authorized paper claimClaim formation — the scientific output itselfcritic Pass required
G4Changing research direction (arm / hypothesis / method)NEW — drift point now that planner authors the plandirector + Pass
Verdict states. Pass proceed · Concern proceed only after the director resolves it · Blocker stop until fixed and re-reviewed. Every feedback item is closed ACCEPTED or REJECTED with rationale — silent skipping is banned.
