The method — CARD-PB v2

An EMA predictor φ guesses each sample's binding norm → full attack budget on that norm, a floor on the others. The floor is adaptive per-norm via miss-volume (no ℓ1 hardcoding — in the de-risk ℓ∞ earned a higher floor than ℓ1, which no hardcoding could produce).

Reactive predecessor = per-sample soft-T=0.25. Both craft with APGD; efficiency = predictive APGD-passes ÷ reactive APGD-passes.
